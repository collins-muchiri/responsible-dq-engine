Method & pledges

rdqe audits survey exports and form definitions with 19 registered rules (R01-R14 for data, F01-F05 for forms plus R09). Each finding carries a severity, the Kenya Data Protection Act (2019) principle and GDPR article it implicates, a remediation note, a planning-level effort estimate in hours, and an estimated cost computed as effort hours × rate (currently KES 2,500.00 per hour). Estimates are planning inputs, not quotations, and the report is not legal advice.

  • Synthetic data only. rdqe ships and audits only obviously invented fixture data. No real survey, beneficiary, client or personal data belongs in this repository or its reports.
  • Local-only. The engine makes no network calls and collects no telemetry at runtime; reports are written to your local disk only.
  • Masked evidence. Personal data found in values is masked (for example 07******78) and sensitive free-text disclosures are reported as matched terms only, never as raw values.
